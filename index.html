Issue 01 · August 2026

We're starting a community bulletin.

A free, easy way for the department to share station news, safety tips, and events with the people we serve — right from your phone.

Reading time: 3 min Cost to run: $0 Where to find it: this QR code, every month

The idea

What this bulletin is

Once a month, Lake McMurray FireHouse will publish a short update for the community — what the department has been doing, safety reminders that matter for the season, upcoming events, and a look at the numbers behind our calls.

No app to download, no account to create. Scan the code on our flyers or apparatus, and you're reading it.

How it works

Built to last, not to be reinvented

  • Every issue gets its own permanent page — this month's is never overwritten by next month's
  • This homepage always shows whatever is newest
  • Every past issue stays visible in the archive
  • The QR code on our flyers points here and will never need to be reprinted

How we're rolling it out

A phased approach

  1. Phase 1 — Starting now Each issue is written up by a volunteer and published here. No coding or technical skill required to contribute content.
  2. Phase 2 — Once it's proven out We add a simple editing tool so any volunteer can publish an issue directly — no single person required to keep it running.

Why this approach

What we gain

  • Free to run, no subscription
  • No coding needed to contribute content
  • A durable, shareable archive of every issue
  • The QR code never has to change
  • Nothing to log in to, download, or sign up for
  • Room to grow as it proves useful

Trade-offs

  • One volunteer publishes each issue at first
  • No push notification — readers come to us
  • A few hours of setup, once
  • No reader stats unless we add analytics later

What we're asking for

Help us make it good

  • Agreement to try this for a few months
  • A volunteer, or a rotation, to write each month's issue
  • Your ideas — what should the community actually see here?
